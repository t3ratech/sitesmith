# Is it really us?

Previews arrive unannounced, so it is right to check. Here is how.

## Every genuine email

- comes from **admin@t3ratech.co.zw**, authenticated for t3ratech.co.zw (your mail app will show it passed SPF and DKIM);
- links your preview, our site (sitesmith.t3ratech.co.zw or t3ratech.co.zw) and, when you choose an option, a WavePay checkout page;

- names Teratech Solutions (Private) Limited, Harare, Zimbabwe.

## We never

- ask for passwords, one-time codes, banking logins or remote access;
- ask you to pay anywhere except a WavePay checkout page;

- call or text you first. We work by email only.

## Still unsure?

Write to [admin@t3ratech.co.zw](mailto:admin@t3ratech.co.zw) from your own email and quote the subject line you received. We answer by email.

## Why the preview is safe to open

Previews are built only from facts your current website already publishes. They are hidden from search engines, so they never compete with your current site, and they are deleted if you reply "no thanks".
