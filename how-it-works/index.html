How it works

From first look to live, usually inside a week.

Four steps

  1. We study your current site

    We read what your website already says (services, people, address, hours) and check how you appear on Google, Google Maps and in AI assistants for the searches your clients make.

  2. We build your new site first

    A complete website in your name, from your own facts. It is private and hidden from search engines while you look at it.

  3. You decide

    If you like it, reply with the option you want and pay the deposit on our WavePay checkout. If not, reply "no thanks" and we delete it.

  4. We launch and make you findable

    The site goes live on your own address, and we do the Visibility Launch: directory listings, social pages, your first posts and search registration.

After launch

On Self-Managed and Signature, your team signs in with a one-time link sent to your email and updates news, people, photos and video. Every change is checked and published automatically. On Essential, email us and we make the change (2 a month included).

If the balance is not paid within 14 days of launch, the site comes down. Nothing else happens, and you owe nothing more.
