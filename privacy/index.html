Privacy

This site counts visits with Google Analytics, but only if you say yes in the bar at the bottom of the page. Until you do, no analytics cookie is set and nothing about your visit is stored. If you say yes, Google Analytics records which pages are read, roughly where from and on what kind of device, with your IP address shortened, and we use it only to see which pages help. We do not use it for advertising. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. Your theme choice, if you make one, also stays in your browser.

Besides Google Analytics, this site loads nothing from other companies.

To build a preview we use what a business publishes about itself: its website, its public listing on Google Maps and its public enquiry email. We use that email to send one message about the preview, and replies to answer you.

Reply "unsubscribe" or "no thanks" to any email and we delete the preview and never write again. Payments are handled by WavePay; we never see card or banking details.
