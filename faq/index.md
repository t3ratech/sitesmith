# Questions

**Why did you build a website for my business before I asked?**

Because it is the fastest way to show what we can do. We used only facts your current website already publishes. The preview is private: it is hidden from search engines and nothing changes on your current site unless you choose to go ahead.

**Is this a scam?**

No. Every email from us comes from admin@t3ratech.co.zw, which is authenticated (SPF, DKIM and DMARC) for t3ratech.co.zw. We never ask for passwords, banking details or remote access, and all payment goes through our WavePay checkout pages. The verify page explains how to check any message that claims to be from us.

**What does it cost?**

Essential $70, Self-Managed $120, Signature $180, in US dollars, wherever you are. You start with a $50 deposit and pay the balance when your site is live. Each option then renews yearly (Essential $20, Self-Managed $25, Signature $30), and the yearly fee covers your web address and hosting.

**How fast is it live?**

Within 3 working days of your deposit and any material you want added. The site is already built, so most of that time is the visibility work.

**What do I get besides the website?**

The Visibility Launch, included with every option: list you in business directories; set up your social pages; write and schedule your first 10 posts; set up your website to be found; add a whatsapp button and an enquiry form; add a news section.

**Can I keep my current domain?**

Yes. The new site goes live on your own web address. We send the one setting your domain needs, or make the change ourselves if you prefer.

**How do I pay?**

Through WavePay checkout pages we send by email: PayNow (EcoCash, OneMoney, InnBucks, ZimSwitch, cards) in Zimbabwe; PayPal (any card or PayPal balance) or USDT on BNB Smart Chain anywhere. The deposit is non-refundable and counts toward the price.

**What if I do not want it?**

Reply "no thanks" and we delete the preview and never write again. If you pay the deposit but not the balance, the site comes down after 14 days.

**Do you work with my competitors?**

One client per kind of business per city. Once a business in your city takes up an option, we do not build for its direct competitors there.
